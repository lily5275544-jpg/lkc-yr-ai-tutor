Case 1: Exertional Shortness of Breath and Systolic Murmur

A 78-year-old man presents with gradually worsening shortness of breath on exertion. He has no chest pain or syncope.

Examination

BP 138/72 mmHg, HR 68/min regular.
Apex beat is not displaced.
There is an ejection systolic murmur loudest at the right upper sternal border, radiating to both carotids.
S2 is soft and the carotid pulse is slow-rising.
There are fine bibasal crepitations.
